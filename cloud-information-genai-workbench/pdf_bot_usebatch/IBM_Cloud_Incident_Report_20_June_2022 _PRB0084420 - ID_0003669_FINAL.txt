I don't have information on the specific outage you're referring to for Amazon AWS, Google GCP, Microsoft Azure, Oracle OCI, Snowflake, Databricks, MongoDB, Akamai, Cloudflare. However, I can provide general information about IBM Cloud.

For IBM Cloud:

* Incident resume: The incident was caused by a power grid utility outage that led to a series of failures in various services.
* Duration of the incident: The outage lasted for approximately 6 hours and 51 minutes (from 03:20 to 10:11).
* Time of the outage: The outage occurred on June 20, 2023, starting at 03:20.
* Impacted services:
	+ VPC (Virtual Private Cloud) - impacted customers experienced an inability to access, manage, and provision VSIs.
	+ SQL Query - SQL jobs/queries experienced long delays and eventually failures for all submitted jobs.
	+ Load Balancer - data plane and control plane for Load Balancers were impacted.
	+ Network Infrastructure - all IBM Cloud services experienced connectivity issues.
	+ VSI (Virtual Server Instance) - impacted customers experienced an outage.
* Impacted regions and availability zones:
	+ Frankfurt (FRA05) Data Center - EU-DE-3 region was affected.
	+ eu-de zone 3 (EU-DE-3)
* Note: The incident did not affect other cloud providers' services, as it was a specific issue with IBM Cloud.
