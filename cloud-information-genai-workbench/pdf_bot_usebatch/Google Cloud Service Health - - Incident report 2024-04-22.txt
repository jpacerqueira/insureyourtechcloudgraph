**Outage Information**

The outage affected Google Cloud Platform (GCP) only.

**Incident Details**

* Incident Start: 22 April 2024 09:00
* Incident End: 22 April 2024 22:18
* Duration: 13 hours, 18 minutes

**Affected Services and Features**

* Google Batch (new batch job creation, scheduling of queued jobs)
* Regions/Zones: us-central1

**Root Cause of Outage**

* Increased transaction contention resulting in significant latency increase.

**Customer Impact**

* Customers from the affected region trying to use Batch experienced delays while changing the status of their batch jobs from Queued to Scheduled.

**Workaround**

* Impacted customers were advised to try submitting their jobs from another region wherever possible.

**Resolution**

* The issue with Batch has been resolved for all affected users as of Monday, 22 April 2024 22:18 US/Pacific Time.
