Here is the information about the outage for Microsoft Azure:

The outage occurred between 03:45 CST and 06:00 CST on April 23, 2024.

The impacted services were:

* Cosmos DB
* Azure Virtual Desktop
* Azure Databricks
* Backup
* Site Recovery
* Azure IoT Hub
* Service Bus
* Logic Apps
* Data Factory
* Azure Kubernetes Services
* Azure Policy
* Azure AI Speech Services

The outage affected the following regions and availability zones in China:

* chinacloudapp.cn domain
* chinacloudsites.cn domain

The root cause of the outage was a configuration change performed through a domain registrar result.

The outage started at 04:59 CST on April 23, 2024.

The outage lasted for approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes (from 04:59 CST to 06:00 CST).

The services that were impacted had a downtime period of approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes.

The outage was marked as resolved at 05:13 CST on April 23, 2024.
