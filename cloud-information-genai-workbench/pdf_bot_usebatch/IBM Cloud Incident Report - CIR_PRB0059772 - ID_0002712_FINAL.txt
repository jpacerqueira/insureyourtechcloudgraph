**Overall Summary**

The outage affected IBM Cloud's WDC01 data center, specifically Pod05, on 06 April 2021 at 08:17. The outage was caused by a failure of the automated power switching logic due to a failure of the dedicated UPS. This resulted in critical load not being transferred from UPS to generator, leading to a partial power outage within the data center.

The outage impacted various services, including Cloudant NoSQL DB, VSI-San Storage, Load Balancers, and API Connect, which were partially impacted during power restoration.
