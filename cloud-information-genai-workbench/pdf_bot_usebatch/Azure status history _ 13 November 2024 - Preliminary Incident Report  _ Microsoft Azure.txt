Here is the resume of the outage:

**Cloud Provider:** Microsoft Azure
**Services Impacted:**

* Azure Log Analytics (West Europe, Southeast Asia, and/or Korea Central regions)
* Azure Container Registry (East US region)

**Root Cause:** Unintentional deletion of Traffic Manager profiles for affected storage accounts.

**Duration:**
- Start Time: 00:50 UTC on November 13, 2024
- End Time: 12:30 UTC on November 13, 2024

**Outage Details:**

* Duration in Days: 1 day
* Duration in Hours: 2 hours
* Duration in Minutes: 30 minutes
* Downtime Period: The affected services experienced DNS resolution failures when interacting with impacted storage accounts in Australia East, Canada Central, Canada East, Central US, East US, East US 2, Japan East, South Central US, UAE North, West US, and/or West US 2.
* Marked as Resolved: Not specified in the text.

Note that Snowflake, MongoDB, Akamai, and Cloudflare are not mentioned in the original text.
