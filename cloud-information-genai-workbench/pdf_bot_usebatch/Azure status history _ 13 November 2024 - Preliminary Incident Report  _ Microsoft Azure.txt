**Resume of the Outage:**

A subset of Azure Blob Storage and Azure Data Lake Storage accounts experienced connectivity errors between 00:50 UTC and 12:30 UTC on November 13, 2024. The issue manifested as loss of access to Blob and Data Lake storage endpoints of affected storage accounts.

**Duration of the Incident:**

The outage lasted for approximately 11 hours and 40 minutes (7400 seconds).

**Impacted Services:**

* Azure Log Analytics
* Azure Container Registry

**Cloud Provider Regions and Availability Zones Impacted:**

The following regions were impacted:

* East US region
* West US region
* Australia East region
* Canada Central region
* Canada East region
* Central US region
* Japan East region
* South Central US region
* UAE North region
* West US 2 region

**Summary of the Outage:**

The outage was caused by an unintentional deletion of Azure Traffic Manager profiles for affected storage accounts. The incident was resolved after the issue was identified and addressed.
