**Summary**

Microsoft Azure was impacted by three outages:

* Outage 1: FIB (Firewall) in South Central US
* Outage 2: Network routers in South Central US
* Outage 3: IPv4 traffic in South Central US and Amsterdam

The root cause of the outages was related to configuration changes that caused denial of service protection to drop a subset of packets.
