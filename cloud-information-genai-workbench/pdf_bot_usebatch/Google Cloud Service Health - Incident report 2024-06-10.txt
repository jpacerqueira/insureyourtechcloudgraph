Here is the breakdown of the outage for each cloud provider:

**Google Cloud**

Resume of Outage:
The outage started on June 10, 2024 at 16:45 PDT and lasted for 3 hours and 15 minutes.

Affected Services and Regions:
- Google BigQuery
- Google Cloud Logging
- US Multi-region

Description of Outage:
BigQuery experienced elevated errors across multiple APIs in the US Multi-region due to the concurrent mitigation of simultaneous degradations, which impacted the BigQuery projects hosted in two degraded clusters. Google will complete a full IR in the following days that will provide a full root cause.

Customer Impact:
- A subset of customers would have experienced HTTP 500 errors while executing calls to insertAll and storage write APIs in the US Multi-region.
- Some customers may have experienced system errors using the Jobs and Query API.
- Cloud Logging customers using Log Analytics faced log ingestion delays up to 2 hours for their analytics buckets if they ingested logs via Cloud regions us-central1 or us-central2.

Root Cause:
The root cause of the outage is not yet known, but Google engineers are working on mitigating the problem and observing the error rate for the impacted APIs dropping.

Downtime Period:
The outage started at 16:45 PDT on June 10, 2024 and ended at 20:00 PDT on June 10, 2024.

**Resolved Date:**
June 10, 2024 at 20:00 PDT

**Summary of Google Cloud Outage:**

| Service | Region/AZ | Duration | Start Time | End Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BigQuery | US Multi-region | 3 hours and 15 minutes | June 10, 2024 at 16:45 PDT | June 10, 2024 at 20:00 PDT |
| Cloud Logging | US Multi-region | Up to 2 hours | June 10, 2024 at 18:07 PDT | June 10, 2024 at 20:09 PDT |

**Overall Summary of Google Cloud Outage:**

Google Cloud experienced an outage on June 10, 2024 that affected BigQuery and Cloud Logging services in the US Multi-region. The outage started at 16:45 PDT and lasted for 3 hours and 15 minutes. Cloud Logging customers faced log ingestion delays up to 2 hours, while some BigQuery customers experienced HTTP 500 errors and system errors using certain APIs.

**Cloud Providers Impacted Services and Regions:**

- Google Cloud
  - Google BigQuery
  - Google Cloud Logging

Note that the outage only affected services in the US Multi-region.
