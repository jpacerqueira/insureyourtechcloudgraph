**Amazon AWS**

The outage occurred in the Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1) Region.

* Services impacted: Support Contact Center, Service Health Dashboard
* Root cause: Persistent congestion and performance issues on networking devices connecting the two networks.
* Duration: 7 hours and 52 minutes
* Start time: 7:33 AM PST
* End time: 2:25 PM PST
* Downtime period: The Support Contact Center was unable to create support cases for 7 hours and 52 minutes.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Overall summary**

The outage in the Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1) Region impacted Amazon AWS services, including the Support Contact Center and Service Health Dashboard. The root cause was persistent congestion and performance issues on networking devices connecting the two networks.

**Root cause analysis**

The teams focused on moving internal DNS traffic away from congested network paths at 9:28 AM PST, which improved the availability of several impacted services by reducing load on the impacted networking devices. However, monitoring data was still not available due to the congestion.
