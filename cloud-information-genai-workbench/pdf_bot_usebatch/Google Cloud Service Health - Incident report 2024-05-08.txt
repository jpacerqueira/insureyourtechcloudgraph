Here is the breakdown of the outage for each cloud provider:

**Google Cloud**

The incident began on May 8, 2024, at 16:41 US/Pacific Time and ended on May 9, 2024, at 03:44 US/Pacific Time.

Affected services:
- Apigee API Platform
- Impacted customers may experience latency in the completion of their API calls

Root cause: Starting Wednesday, May 8, 2024, customers making use of caching policies in their API proxies were experiencing issues with Apigee API Platform services.

Duration: The outage lasted for approximately 2 hours and 3 minutes.

Downtime period:
- May 8, 2024, 16:41 US/Pacific Time to May 9, 2024, 03:44 US/Pacific Time

Resolved on: May 9, 2024, at 03:40 US/Pacific Time
