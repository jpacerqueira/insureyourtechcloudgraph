**Root Cause**

The third-party vendor applied changes to their BGP configuration, which caused a critical set of IP prefixes to be withdrawn. This led to a loss of connectivity for IBM Cloud Internet Services.

**Timeline**

* 21 June 2022 06:27 - The third-party vendor applied changes to their BGP configuration.
* 21 June 2022 06:34 - IBM Cloud Specialists were alerted to HTTP 500 errors on their applications protected by Internet Services. Incident started.
* 21 June 2022 06:44 - The third-party vendor notified IBM about a Global outage on their global PoPs.
* 21 June 2022 07:42 - The third-party vendor reverted all changes.
* 21 June 2022 08:06 - Customers and service teams reported restoration of services. Incident ended.
