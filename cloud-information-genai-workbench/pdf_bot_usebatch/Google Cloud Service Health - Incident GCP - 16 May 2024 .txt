**Google Cloud (continued)**

* Applications that retry with a different TCP source port would have experienced limited outages even in these Cloud zones.
* Long-running VMs were also affected due to data plane overload in a few locations.

Summary:

* Google Cloud was impacted by a service outage on May 16, 2024, at 09:44 PDT.
* The outage lasted for 2 hours and 48 minutes, with some services taking longer to recover.
* The root cause of the outage was a bug in automation that caused components to be shut down in all zones where they were still in use.
* The downtime period affected multiple Google Cloud products across various regions and availability zones.
