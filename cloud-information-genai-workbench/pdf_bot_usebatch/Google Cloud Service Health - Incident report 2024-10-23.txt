**Google Cloud**

The incident began on October 23, 2024, at 18:22 US/Pacific Time and ended on October 24, 2024, at 02:01 US/Pacific Time.

The impacted services were:
- Google Cloud Dataflow
- Google Cloud Dataproc
- Google Cloud Pub/Sub
- Vertex AI Batch Prediction
- Cloud SQL

The root cause of the outage was a misconfiguration and triggered by the outage in europe-west3-c. To prevent a recurrence of this issue, engineers have hardened this component against zonal outages in europe-west3.

The downtime period for each service was:
- Google Cloud Dataflow: 19:05 US/Pacific Time to 23:55 US/Pacific Time (6 hours and 50 minutes)
- Google Cloud Dataproc: 19:12 US/Pacific Time to 22:00 US/Pacific Time (2 hours and 48 minutes)
- Google Cloud Pub/Sub: 18:25 US/Pacific Time to 18:41 US/Pacific Time (16 minutes) and elevated latency for push subscriptions from 22:57 US/Pacific Time to 00:11 US/Pacific Time (1 hour and 14 minutes)
- Vertex AI Batch Prediction: 19:45 US/Pacific Time to 23:30 US/Pacific Time (3 hours and 45 minutes)
- Cloud SQL: 18:35 US/Pacific Time to 24 October 2024, 23:28 US/Pacific Time (5 hours and 53 minutes)

The outage was marked as resolved on October 24, 2024.
