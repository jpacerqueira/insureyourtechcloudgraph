Here is the information about the outage on Amazon AWS:

**Outage Details:**

* **Services Impacted:** Amazon CloudWatch Logs, Amazon Data Firehose, Amazon Elastic Container Service (ECS), AWS Lambda, Amazon Redshift, and AWS Glue
* **Regions Affected:** Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1) Region
* **Availability Zones Affected:** Not specified

**Root Cause of Outage:**
The outage was caused by a degradation of one of the internally-used cells of the Amazon Kinesis Data Streams service.

**Duration of Outage:**

* **Start Time:** 2:45 PM PDT
* **End Time:** 9:37 PM PDT
* **Duration:** 7 hours and 52 minutes
* **Days:** 1 day

**Downtime Period:**
The outage started at 2:45 PM PDT on July 30th, 2024, and lasted for approximately 8 hours.

**Resolution Time:**
Normal operations were restored to the Kinesis Data Streams cell by 7:21 PM PDT, which marked the end of the outage. The backlog of delayed S3 events completed processing at 2:38 AM PDT on August 1st.
