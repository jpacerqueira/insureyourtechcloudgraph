**Summary**

The outage affected services in the Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1) Region. The impacted services included CloudWatch Logs, Amazon Data Firehose, event framework that publishes Amazon S3 events, Amazon Elastic Container Service (ECS), AWS Lambda, Amazon Redshift, and AWS Glue.

**Overall Summary**

Amazon AWS experienced an outage in the Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1) Region. The outage was caused by a degradation of one of our internally-used cells of the Amazon Kinesis Data Streams service. The outage lasted for 6 hours and 52 minutes, with the downtime period starting at 2:38 AM PDT on August 1st and ending at 9:37 PM PDT.
