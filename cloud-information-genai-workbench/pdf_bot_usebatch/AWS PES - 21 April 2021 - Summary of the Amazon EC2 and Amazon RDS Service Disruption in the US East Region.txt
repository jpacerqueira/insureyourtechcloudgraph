I can provide information on the Amazon AWS outage, as the other cloud providers are not mentioned. Here's a summary of the outage:

**Incident Details:**

* The incident started with an EBS cluster degradation issue in the US East Region.
* The outage affected EC2 instances and RDS databases hosted in the primary impacted Availability Zone (AZ).
* The issue caused "stuck" volumes, leading to elevated error rates and latencies for new EBS-backed EC2 launches.

**Duration of Outage:**

* The outage started at an unspecified time before April 21st.
* The outage lasted for approximately 11 hours, from the onset until Noon PM PDT on April 21st.
* After the initial 11-hour period, the issue was partially resolved when a change to the EBS control plane API fixed the problem.

**Impacted Services and Regions:**

* Impacted services:
	+ Amazon EC2 instances (EBS-backed)
	+ Amazon RDS databases
* Affected regions:
	+ US East Region

**Availability Zones Impacted:**

* The primary impacted Availability Zone was not specified, but it is mentioned that the outage affected a single AZ.

**Resolution and Recovery:**

* The outage was partially resolved at 11:30 AM PDT when a change to the EBS control plane API fixed the issue.
* After the initial resolution, customers with automatic backups turned on had the option to initiate point-in-time database restore operations for their RDS instances.
* By April 24th, most of the affected volumes had been recovered, but some remained "stuck" due to machine failure.
