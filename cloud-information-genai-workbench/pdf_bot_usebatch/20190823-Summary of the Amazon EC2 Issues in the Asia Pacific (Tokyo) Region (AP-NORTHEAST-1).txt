Based on the provided text, I will resume the outage for Amazon AWS.

**Outage Details:**

* **Cloud Provider:** Amazon AWS
* **Incident:** A small percentage of EC2 servers in a single Availability Zone (AZ) in the Tokyo (AP-NORTHEAST-1) Region shut down due to overheating.
* **Start Time:** 12:36 PM JST on August 23, 2019
* **Duration:** The outage lasted for approximately 3 hours and 45 minutes.

**Impacted Services and Regions/AZs:**

* Amazon EC2 in the Tokyo (AP-NORTHEAST-1) Region, specifically in a single Availability Zone.
* Amazon EBS resources in the affected area of the Availability Zone.
* Some other services, such as RDS, Redshift, ElastiCache, and Workspaces, would have seen some impact if their underlying EC2 instances were affected.

**Root Cause:**
The root cause of the outage was a control system failure that caused multiple, redundant cooling systems to fail in parts of the affected Availability Zone.

**Downtime Period:**

* The affected cooling systems were restored at 3:21 PM JST.
* Temperatures in the affected areas began to return to normal after the cooling systems were restored.
* Power was restored to the affected instances by 6:30 PM JST.
* A small number of instances and volumes hosted on hardware that was adversely affected by the loss of power and excessive heat took longer to recover.

**Resolved Time:** The outage was marked as resolved at 6:30 PM JST on August 23, 2019.
