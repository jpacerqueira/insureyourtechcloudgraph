**Google Cloud Service**

The issue with Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) customers located in us-central1 may observe an issue with VPC Service Controls for newly created VMs or networks.

* **Service Impacted:** Virtual Private Cloud (VPC)
* **Region/Availability Zone:** us-central1
* **Impact Date:** 2024-09-04 06:29 US/Pacific Time
* **Duration:** 6 hours and 10 minutes
* **Root Cause:** VPC network data for newly created VMs or networks might not be available when requests to Google APIs originate from these VMs/networks.
* **Downtime Period:** The issue was causing VPC Service Control restrictions to deny requests for clients that should otherwise be allowed. This may also affect newly created VMs or networks that are non-RFC1918 private networks from using Private Google Access.
* **Resolved Date:** 2024-09-04 11:06 US/Pacific Time

**Summary of Outage**

Google Cloud Service was impacted by an outage in the us-central1 region. The outage started on September 4, 2024, at 6:29 AM Pacific Time and lasted for approximately 6 hours and 10 minutes. The root cause of the outage was related to VPC network data not being available for newly created VMs or networks.
