The outage occurred in the Tokyo (AP-NORTHEAST-1) Region of Amazon AWS on September 2, 2021. The incident started at 7:30 AM JST and was resolved by 12:30 PM JST and then fully resolved by 1:42 PM JST.

The impacted services were Direct Connect customers connecting to the Tokyo Region. The outage affected connectivity issues and elevated packet loss for these customers.

The root cause of the outage was the failure of a subset of network devices on one of the network layers along the network path from Direct Connect edge locations to the Datacenter network in the Tokyo Region, where customers' Virtual Private Clouds (VPCs) reside.

The outage lasted for approximately 5 hours and 12 minutes. The downtime period started at 7:30 AM JST and ended at 1:42 PM JST.
