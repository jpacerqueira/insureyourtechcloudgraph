Here is the summary of the outage:

**Summary**

The outage affected Google Cloud Platform (GCP) services in the us-east5-c region.

**Services Impacted**

* AlloyDB for PostgreSQL
* BigQuery
* Cloud Bigtable
* Cloud Composer
* Cloud Dataflow
* Cloud Filestore
* Cloud Identity and Access Management
* Cloud Interconnect
* Cloud Kubernetes Engine
* Persistent Disk

The outage started on March 29, 2025, at 12:53 UTC and lasted for 6 hours and 19 minutes.

**Root Cause**

The root cause of the outage was a utility power outage in the zone and a subsequent failure of batteries within the uninterruptible power supply (UPS) system supporting a portion of the impacted zone.

**Downtime Period**

The downtime period started on March 29, 2025, at 12:53 UTC and ended on March 30, 2025, at 19:12 UTC.

**Resolved Date**

The outage was marked as resolved on March 30, 2025, at 17:30 UTC.
