**Azure Status History**

* Outage: approximately 20:30 - 00:30 UTC on March 15, 2020
* Duration: 2 hours (0 days, 2 hours)
* Impacted services: Customers in West Central US region.
* Root cause: Ice storm in the region resulted in a loss of power to both primary and secondary utility power feeds in West Central US. This caused the datacenter to transition to generator power. Although largely successful, a subset of generators failed and UPS units supporting the downstream racks carried the load until their batteries drained. At that point, the affected racks lost power entirely. Any customer resources hosted on the affected racks became unavailable at the time.
* Start time: approximately 20:30 UTC on March 15, 2020
* Resolution time: Not specified
