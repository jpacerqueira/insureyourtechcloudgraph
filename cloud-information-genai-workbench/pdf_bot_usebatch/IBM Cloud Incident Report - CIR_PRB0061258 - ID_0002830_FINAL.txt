**Outage Information**

The outage affected the following cloud providers: IBM Cloud, Private DNS, and Cloudant.

**IBM Cloud Services Impacted**

* HyperProtect-VS: Impacted customers experienced provisioning issues in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.
* MqCloud: Impacted customers were unable to provision new or access existing instances of MQ on Cloud in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.
* Mobile Foundation: Impacted customers were unable to upgrade/update the configuration of their service instances in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.
* Hyper Protect DBaaS: Impacted customers experienced provisioning failures in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.

**Private DNS Services Impacted**

* Private DNS resolution timeouts from IKS clusters in VPC in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.
* Private hostname lookups for VSIs on VPC were not successful in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.
* Any service that uses a Virtual Private Endpoint in VPC was unavailable in Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South regions.

**Cloudant Services Impacted**

* Cloudant services returned to normal state on 26 May 2021 at 01:40 UTC after being restricted for 12 hours and 7 minutes.

**Root Cause of Outage**

The root cause of the outage was an abnormally high burst of Private DNS traffic that caused resource constraints in the Cloudant HA Proxy Load Balancers, leading to service impact due to congestion on these devices.

**Outage Timeline**

* 25 May 2021 13:33 UTC: Incident started
* 25 May 2021 14:20 UTC: Affected clusters were identified
* 25 May 2021 15:16 UTC: Disruptive traffic was identified
* 25 May 2021 15:51 UTC: Isolation and removal of Private DNS traffic allowed cluster traffic to restart in Sydney
* 25 May 2021 16:09 UTC: Service recovery in Sydney was confirmed and isolation of traffic continued for other clusters
* 26 May 2021 01:28 UTC: Rate-limited updates were rolled out across all affected regions
* 26 May 2021 01:40 UTC: Cloudant services returned to normal state

**Outage Summary**

The outage lasted for 12 hours and 7 minutes, starting on 25 May 2021 at 13:33 UTC. The root cause of the outage was an abnormally high burst of Private DNS traffic that caused resource constraints in the Cloudant HA Proxy Load Balancers.
