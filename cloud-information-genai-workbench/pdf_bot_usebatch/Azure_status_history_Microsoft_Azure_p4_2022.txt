Here is the summary of the outage:

**Cloud Provider:** Microsoft Azure
**Outage Details:**

* **Incident Start Date and Time:** August 27 at 02:47 UTC
* **Duration:** The outage lasted for approximately 1 day, with a brief period of recovery in the morning.
* **Affected Regions and Availability Zones:** The West US 2 region, which includes multiple datacenters across three Availability Zones on two campuses.
* **Impacted Services:**
	+ Azure Virtual Machines (VMs) running Ubuntu 18.04
	+ Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)
	+ Azure Monitor
	+ Application Insights
	+ Log Analytics
	+ Microsoft Sentinel
* **Root Cause:** A bug in Ubuntu, specifically the unattended-upgrade package, which caused DNS resolution errors.
* **Downtime Period:**
	+ The outage started at 02:47 UTC on August 27 and lasted for approximately 12 hours.
	+ The downtime period was marked as resolved around 14:47 UTC on August 28.
