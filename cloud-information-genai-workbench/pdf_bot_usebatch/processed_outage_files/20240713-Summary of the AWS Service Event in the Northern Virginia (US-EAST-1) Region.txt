**Summary**

The outage occurred in Amazon AWS (US-EAST-1 Region) on June 13th, 2023.

**Services Impacted**

* Lambda function invocations
* Amazon STS
* AWS Management Console
* Amazon EKS
* Amazon Connect
* Amazon EventBridge

**Root Cause**

A latent software defect in the provisioning of underlying compute capacity.

**Duration and Downtime Period**

The outage started at 11:49 AM PDT and lasted for approximately 3 hours and 48 minutes. The downtime period was from 11:49 AM PDT to 3:37 PM PDT.
