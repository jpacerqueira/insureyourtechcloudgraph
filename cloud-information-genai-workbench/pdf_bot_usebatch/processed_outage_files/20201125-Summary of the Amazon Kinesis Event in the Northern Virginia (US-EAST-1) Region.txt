Here is the information about the outage:

**Summary of Outage**

The outage occurred on November 25th, 2020, and affected several services across Amazon AWS.

**Services Impacted**

* Amazon Kinesis
* Lambda
* CloudWatch Events and EventBridge

**Outage Details**

**Amazon Kinesis**

* Region/Availability Zone: Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1)
* Impact Date: November 25th, 2020, at 2:44 AM PST
* Duration: Approximately 5 hours and 47 minutes
* Root Cause: A relatively small addition of capacity that began to be added to the service
* Downtime Period: The outage started at 2:44 AM PST and ended at 8:31 AM PST
* Resolved Date: November 25th, 2020, at 5:47 PM PST

**Lambda**

* Region/Availability Zone: Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1)
* Impact Date: November 25th, 2020, at 6:15 AM PST
* Duration: Approximately 4 hours and 21 minutes
* Root Cause: Memory contention on the underlying service hosts used for Lambda function invocations
* Downtime Period: The outage started at 6:15 AM PST and ended at 10:36 AM PST
* Resolved Date: November 25th, 2020, at 10:36 AM PST

**CloudWatch Events and EventBridge**

* Region/Availability Zone: Northern Virginia (US-EAST-1)
* Impact Date: November 25th, 2020, at 5:15 AM PST
* Duration: Approximately 4 hours and 32 minutes
* Root Cause: Increased API errors and delays in event processing due to the outage of Kinesis
* Downtime Period: The outage started at 5:15 AM PST and ended at 9:47 AM PST
* Resolved Date: November 25th, 2020, at 9:47 AM PST
