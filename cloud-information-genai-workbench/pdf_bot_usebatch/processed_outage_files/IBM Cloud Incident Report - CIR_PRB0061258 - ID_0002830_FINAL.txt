Here is the information about the outage:

**IBM Cloud**

* Services impacted: Private DNS, HyperProtect-VS, MqCloud, Mobile Foundation, Hyper Protect DBaaS
* Regions/Availability Zones: Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, US-South
* Impact Date: 25 May 2021 13:33 UTC
* Duration: 12 hours 7 minutes
* Root Cause: Resource constraints in Cloudant HA Proxy Load Balancers caused by increased database queries from Private DNS
* Downtime Period: 25 May 2021 01:40 - 26 May 2021 01:40 UTC
* Resolved Date: 26 May 2021 01:40 UTC

**Sequence of Events**

* 25 May 2021 13:33 - IBM Cloudant Specialists received alerts from multiple system areas indicating timeouts and connection loss. Incident started.
* 25 May 2021 14:20 - Affected clusters were identified.
* 25 May 2021 15:16 - Disruptive traffic was identified. Isolation activity began for affected clusters.
* 25 May 2021 15:51 - Isolation and removal of Private DNS traffic allowed cluster traffic to restart in Sydney.
* 25 May 2021 16:09 - Service recovery in Sydney was confirmed and isolation of traffic continued for other clusters.
* 26 May 2021 01:28 - Rate-limited updates were rolled out across all affected regions. Limited service resumed.
* 26 May 2021 01:40 - Cloudant services returned to normal state. Incident ended.

**Summary**

The outage impacted IBM Cloud services in multiple regions and availability zones, including Frankfurt, London, Tokyo, Sydney, US-East, and US-South. The root cause of the outage was resource constraints in Cloudant HA Proxy Load Balancers caused by increased database queries from Private DNS. The outage lasted for 12 hours and 7 minutes, with a downtime period of 12 hours and 7 minutes.
