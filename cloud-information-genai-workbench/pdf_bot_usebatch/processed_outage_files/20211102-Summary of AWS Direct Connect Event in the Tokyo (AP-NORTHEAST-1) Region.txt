**Summary of Outage**

The outage occurred in the Tokyo (AP-NORTHEAST-1) Region on September 2, 2021. The Direct Connect service experienced intermittent connectivity issues and elevated packet loss for customers connecting to the Tokyo Region.

**Cloud Providers Impacted**

* Amazon AWS
* Google GCP is not mentioned in the text
