The outage occurred on June 20, 2023, at 03:49 UTC and lasted for 18 hours and 26 minutes.

**Root Cause of the Outage**

The final root cause of the generator failure was a failed relay on the generator control system, resulting in a soft (partial) failure scenario that impeded handover between the UPS (battery) and generator systems.

**Outage Timeline**

Here is the outage timeline for each service:

* 03:49 UTC - Incident started
* 04:12 UTC - IBM Cloud Site Operator Engineering team began data center recovery actions
* 04:17 UTC - Power was restored on the affected rack
* 04:30 UTC - All bare metal hosts were back online
* 05:00 UTC - Facilities grid power supply was restored and transition from generator to utility was completed
* 05:41 UTC - IBM Cloud Site Operator Engineering team notified that power was restored after inspection
* 10:33 UTC - VPC underlying host operating system was restored
* 12:18 UTC - All VSI Hosts were recovered
* 19:14 UTC - VPC SDN was restored
